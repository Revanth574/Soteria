What actually costs you money

Three clauses from the policy wording, in the order they're most likely to bite.

Room rent cap, with proportionate deduction

Critical

If you take a hospital room above what your Sum Insured tier allows — a single standard A/C room for ₹5L+ policies, tighter rupee caps below that — the insurer doesn't just refuse the extra room cost. It cuts your payout on nearly everything else in the bill: surgeon fees, operation theatre charges, consultant fees, all reduced by the same proportion your room breached its limit.

you have a ₹5L policy (limit: standard A/C room) and take a suite, the insurer can reduce a ₹1L surgeon-fee payout by 40% because of the room breach alone.

Estimated exposure: 20–40% of the total hospital bill, out of pocket.

Verified against the wording

Expenses relating to Associated medical expenses will be considered in proportion to the eligible room rent/room category stated in the policy schedule or actuals whichever is less.

Coverage > In-patient Treatment

Cataract and modern-treatment limits capped well below real cost

High

Cataract surgery is capped by a fixed rupee table tied to your Sum Insured tier — not a percentage that scales with what you paid for. A ₹5L policy still only pays up to ₹40,000 per eye. Several modern treatments (robotic surgery, balloon sinuplasty, and others) are capped the same way.

cataract surgery in a tier-1 city costs ₹65,000 and your policy caps the payout at ₹40,000, you pay the ₹25,000 difference yourself — on a policy you may have bought assuming a bigger Sum Insured meant bigger coverage everywhere.

Estimated exposure: ₹20,000–50,000 per procedure, out of pocket.

Verified against the wording

Expenses incurred on treatment of Cataract is subject to the limit as per the following table... Up to ₹40,000/- per eye.

Coverage > Treatment of Cataract / Modern Treatments

Mandatory 20% co-pay if you join at 61 or older

High

This isn't an optional discount-for-co-pay trade — if your entry age is 61 or above, the 20% co-pay is compulsory, on every claim, for as long as you hold the policy.

you're 65 and hospitalized with an approved ₹10 lakh claim, the insurer pays 80% and you cover the remaining ₹2,00,000 yourself, regardless of your Sum Insured.

Estimated exposure: 20% of every approved claim, permanently.

Verified against the wording

This policy is subject to co-payment of 20% of each and every claim amount for fresh as well as renewal policies for Insured Persons whose age at the time of entry is 61 years and above.

Your caps, by Sum Insured

Room rent and cataract limits don't scale in step with what you pay. Buying a bigger policy buys you more Sum Insured — not proportionally more freedom on either of these two clauses.

The gap: going from a ₹1L to a ₹10L policy is a 10× increase in Sum Insured. Your cataract coverage rises only about 4×, from ₹12,000 to ₹50,000 per eye. The room rent clause has the same shape — it eases from a hard rupee cap to a room-category description, but never removes the link between your room choice and how much of the rest of your bill gets paid.

What this gets right

The base policy isn't all traps — several defaults here are genuinely better than the market standard.

✓

No mandatory deductible. Unlike many top-up-style products, you don't need to clear a fixed amount yourself before the policy starts paying.

✓

Day-care isn't restricted to a named list. "All Day Care Treatments" are covered — a real advantage over policies that only pay for a fixed, enumerated set of procedures.

✓

Standard pre-existing-disease waiting period. 36 months — the ceiling IRDAI allows, not longer, which some retail plans quietly extend.

✓

Guaranteed lifetime renewability, no age cap. The policy won't be withdrawn from you as you get older, barring fraud or misrepresentation.

✓

Automatic restoration, 3 times at 100%. If you exhaust your Sum Insured on one claim, it restores in full for unrelated illnesses later the same year — for SI ₹3L and above.

✓

Loyalty bonus up to 100% of your Sum Insured. Claim-free years compound at 10% annually, for SI ₹3L and above.
