A strong top-up — but it pays nothing until you clear the deductible.

Supreme Enhance is a super top-up plan, not a standalone health policy. Once your cumulative claims for the year cross your chosen deductible, the coverage underneath is genuinely broad: unlimited restoration that covers even the same illness twice, no ICU cap, and modern treatments paid up to your full Sum Insured. The part worth understanding clearly, before you buy, is exactly how that deductible clears.

What actually costs you money

One clause is how this type of policy is designed to work. The other two are worth knowing before you're in a hospital bed.

The aggregate annual deductible — this policy's core mechanic

High

This is a top-up policy: it doesn't pay from the first rupee. Your approved medical bills for the year must cumulatively cross your chosen deductible — anywhere from ₹1L to ₹25L — before Supreme Enhance contributes anything. Every claim in the policy year counts toward that same running total.

your deductible is ₹5L and you have a ₹3L claim, you pay the full ₹3L yourself; a second ₹3L claim later the same year only needs ₹2L more to clear the deductible, so the insurer pays the remaining ₹1L.

You are responsible for 100% of bills until the deductible is cumulatively cleared for the year.

Verified against the wording

Deductible amount (as opted) applicable on aggregate basis on claims in a policy year. (Plan A)

Room rent cap, with proportionate deduction

High

The base plan restricts you to a Single Private AC room. Choose anything above that category and the insurer proportionately cuts payouts across nearly every other line item on the bill, not just the room charge.

you upgrade to a suite and the insurer pays the room up to the Private AC limit, but also cuts your ₹2L surgeon bill by 30–40% because of the same room breach.

Estimated exposure: 20–50% of the total bill, depending on the room-rate gap.

Verified against the wording

If a room accommodation has been opted for where the Room Rent or Room Category is higher than the eligible limit as applicable for that Insured Person as specified, then, the Associate Medical Expenses payable shall be pro-rated as per the applicable limits.

General Conditions > Claim Assessment 6.6

Modern-treatment sub-limit — only if you opt in for the discount

HighOpt-in only

The base policy covers advanced treatments (robotic surgery, stem cell therapy, and similar) up to your full Sum Insured. If you choose the "Modification on Advance Technology methods" add-on to lower your premium, you introduce a hard cap — 30% or 50% of SI — on exactly those treatments.

you have a ₹20L policy, opted for the discount, and need robotic surgery costing ₹12L, a 50%-of-SI cap limits payout to ₹10L, leaving ₹2L for you to cover.

Estimated exposure: ₹1L–5L, only if this optional cover was selected and an advanced treatment is needed.

Verified against the wording

If this Optional Benefit is opted, then there shall be sub-limit on Advance Technology Method treatments up to the limit and our liability shall be limited to such extent... Coverage shall be limited to 30%/50% of SI.

Note: the deductible is annual — it resets each policy year. A large bill that lands close to your renewal date could mean starting the count over again, even if you'd nearly cleared it the year before.

What this gets right

Once the deductible clears, the coverage underneath is unusually broad for this price tier.

✓

No mandatory co-pay. Nothing reduces your payout by a fixed percentage once the deductible is cleared.

✓

No ICU cap at all. Intensive care is covered with no limit, base plan.

✓

Modern treatments covered up to full Sum Insured in the base plan — unless you opt into the discount that caps them.

✓

Unlimited automatic restoration, and unlike most policies, it covers the same illness again, not just unrelated ones.

✓

Loyalty bonus doesn't shrink after a claim — 10% of SI per year, up to 100%, regardless of claims history.

✓

Standard PED wait, with a paid option to shorten it — 36 months by default, reducible to 1–2 years via an add-on.
